About

Bangladesh's trusted partner for software-defined infrastructure

Who We Are

World-class platforms, delivered locally

MetaVirt Information Technologies Private Limited is a Bangladesh-based enterprise infrastructure company. We help organizations modernize their IT infrastructure with private cloud, virtualization, storage, backup, and container platforms — powered by proven, globally recognized software.

As a local delivery and support partner, we bring internationally developed platforms to the Bangladesh market and make them run seamlessly on your choice of commodity servers. You get enterprise-grade capability without expensive proprietary lock-in, backed by a team that understands local compliance and connectivity.

Our team combines deep technical expertise with local market understanding to design, deploy, and support infrastructure solutions that meet the unique requirements of Bangladeshi enterprises.
